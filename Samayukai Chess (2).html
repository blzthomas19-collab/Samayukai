        Three games on one mandala

        A 9×9 board with a shaded 3×3 mandala in the middle and the lotus square (e5) at its heart. Any piece except the King may stand on the lotus. Each side has a King, five great animals that each carry an element colour (Eagle: sky, violet · Rhino: water, blue · Owl: air, teal · Tiger: fire, red · Elephant: earth, green), two Chariots, two Horses and nine Soldiers. The animals are compact shapes that carve easily from one block of wood. Pieces move like chess unless their card says otherwise.

        Pick a game

        Samayukai Chess

        The main game.

        • Win by checkmate.
        • The King may never step onto the lotus square, so it can't hide in the centre.
        Animal Hunt

        A race to capture the enemy's five great animals.

        • Win by capturing all five enemy great animals (Eagle, Rhino, Owl, Tiger, Elephant).
        • Checkmate still wins too.
        • Soldiers bring captured animals back, so the hunt can swing.
        Lotus Hold

        Hold the centre with any animal.

        • Put any piece on the lotus square.
        • If it is still there when your next turn begins, you win.
        • Checkmate still wins. The King can't be the holder.

        Soldier rebirth

        A Soldier that reaches the far rank turns into one of your captured great animals. If all five are still on the board, it becomes a Chariot. A Soldier may step two squares from its starting rank. There is no castling and no en passant.

        Draw rules

        Stalemate

        The player to move has no legal move and is not in check.

        Threefold repetition

        The same position, with the same player to move, appears for the third time.

        100-move rule

        100 half-moves (50 by each player) pass with no capture and no Soldier move.

        Too few pieces

        Neither side can checkmate: each has only a King, or a King and one Horse. In Lotus Hold this applies only when both sides have just a King, since any other piece can still hold the lotus.

        Agreement

        One player offers a draw and the other accepts. The computer accepts only when it is losing.
